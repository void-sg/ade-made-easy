TRACK 2 • MEMORY, TIMING & STATE MACHINES

Sequential Logic Circuits & Systems

The 6 Essential Pillars

  • Pillar 1: Foundations & 1-Bit Memory Storage Combinational vs. Sequential, Level vs. Edge triggering, S-R latches, and async Preset/Clear overrides.
  • Pillar 2: The Core 4 Flip-Flops & Internal Physics Clocked SR, JK toggle state, Race-Around condition ($t_p > \Delta t$), and Master-Slave JK (IC 7476).
  • Pillar 3: Excitation Tables & Flip-Flop Conversions The Master Excitation Table with Don't Cares ($X$), and Universal 3-Step Flip-Flop Conversions.
  • Pillar 4: Shift Registers & IC 74194 The 4 operating modes (SISO, SIPO, PISO, PIPO), IC 74194 Universal SR, Ring and Johnson Counters.
  • Pillar 5: Counters (Ripple vs. Synchronous) Ripple delay accumulation ($f_{max}$ formulas), IC 7490 Decade/Mod-6, IC 7493 Binary/Mod-12.
  • Pillar 6: Finite State Machines & Real-World Physics Mealy/Moore models, 1010 sequence detector, VLSI timing (setup/hold/metastability), and switch debouncers.
