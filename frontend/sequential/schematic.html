IC 74LS90 Decade / BCD Counter Architecture

1. Asynchronous Dual Section Structure
Contains a divide-by-two section (clocked by CP0 into QA) and a divide-by-five section (clocked by CP1 into QB, QC, QD). Jumpering QA to CP1 produces a complete BCD Mod-10 decade counter (0 to 9).
2. Mod-6 Digital Clock Synthesis
To build a Mod-6 counter (minutes/seconds tens digit: 0..5), outputs QC (Pin 8) and QB (Pin 9) are tied back to reset inputs R₀(1) and R₀(2). At state 6 (0110), the gated reset instantly clears the counter back to 0000.

IC 74LS93 4-Bit Binary Ripple Counter Architecture

1. Cascaded Divide-by-2 and Divide-by-8
Clock CP0 drives the LSB flip-flop QA. Connecting QA to CP1 cascades the divide-by-8 section (QB, QC, QD), producing a 4-bit binary ripple counter cycling from 0000 (0) to 1111 (15).
2. Mod-12 Truncated Counter Reset
By feeding back QD (Pin 11) and QC (Pin 8) into the active-high reset-to-zero gates R₀(1) and R₀(2), the counter automatically resets upon reaching decimal 12 (binary 1100), producing a standard Mod-12 counter (0..11).

IC 74LS74 Dual D-Type Positive-Edge-Triggered Flip-Flop Architecture

1. Data Storage & Edge Triggering
On the low-to-high transition (↑) of the clock pulse (CP), data present at D is transferred to output Q ($Q_{n+1} = D$). Setup and hold timing constraints must be respected before and after the edge.
2. Asynchronous Overrides
Active-Low Preset (~PR) and Clear (~CLR) override clock and data. If ~PR=0, Q is forced to 1. If ~CLR=0, Q is forced to 0. Setting both ~PR=~CLR=0 creates a forbidden indeterminate condition.

IC 74LS76 Dual Master-Slave J-K Flip-Flop Architecture

1. Eliminating the Race-Around Condition
Two internal latches (Master and Slave) isolate input sampling from output switching. The Master samples J and K while the clock pulse is high, and the Slave updates the outputs Q and ~Q on the falling edge (↓).
2. The Toggle State (J=1, K=1)
Unlike SR flip-flops where S=R=1 is forbidden, the JK flip-flop inverts its output state on every clock pulse ($Q_{n+1} = \overline{Q}_n$), serving as the fundamental building block for frequency dividers and binary ripple counters.

IC 74LS194 4-Bit Bidirectional Universal Shift Register Architecture

1. 4 Operational Modes via Mode Select
The control lines S₁ and S₀ determine register behavior: 00 = Hold (no clock effect), 01 = Shift Right ($Q_A \leftarrow SR\_IN$), 10 = Shift Left ($Q_D \leftarrow SL\_IN$), 11 = Parallel Load ($Q_A..Q_D \leftarrow A..D$).
2. Multi-Purpose System Roles
Implements SISO (digital delay line), SIPO (UART serial receiver), PISO (UART serial transmitter), PIPO (high-speed CPU data bus buffer), Ring Counters, and Johnson (Twisted-Ring) Counters.
